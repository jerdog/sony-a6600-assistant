# Settings Profiles (Sony a6600)

Baselines to start from — adjust for the actual lens (max aperture, OSS)
and conditions. When the user asks to save a custom variant, add it here
as a new entry (or in their `/topics/camera-gear.md` memory file if this
skill's files are read-only post-install).

## Portrait

- Mode: A (Aperture Priority) or M
- Aperture: f/1.8–f/2.8 (wider for more background separation, narrower
  if shooting groups)
- Shutter: 1/160s+ handheld, or per handheld-focal-length rule
- ISO: Auto, cap around 3200 outdoors / 6400 indoors
- AF: AF-S, Eye AF (Human) on, Focus Area: Tracking or Spot
- Drive: Single or Lo continuous for slight variation in expression
- Other: Face/Eye priority in AF menu; consider a slight minus exposure
  compensation (-0.3 to -0.7) to protect highlights on skin

## Action / Wildlife

- Mode: S (Shutter Priority) or M
- Shutter: 1/1000s–1/2000s depending on subject speed
- Aperture: widest usable that still gets the subject sharp (often
  f/5.6–f/8 on telephoto zooms at max reach)
- ISO: Auto, cap raised to 6400–12800 if needed to hold shutter speed
- AF: AF-C, Wide or Zone tracking; Real-time Tracking for erratic subjects
- Drive: Hi or Hi+ continuous
- Other: Consider electronic shutter for higher fps if no flicker/rolling-
  shutter risk; back-button focus recommended for action

## Low-light / Astro

- Mode: M
- Aperture: widest available on the lens
- Shutter: per the crop-adjusted 500 rule for stars (500 / effective
  focal length); much slower and on a tripod for static night scenes
- ISO: 1600–3200 starting point for astro; adjust after chimping histogram
- AF: Manual focus, magnified live view on a bright star/light for infinity
- Drive: Single, 2s timer or remote trigger to avoid shake
- Other: IBIS off on tripod; consider a star-tracker for long astro
  exposures if available

## Video (general handheld)

- Mode: Movie mode, M or Auto exposure per preference
- Shutter: roughly 1/(2x frame rate) for natural motion blur (e.g.
  1/60s at 30fps)
- Aperture: per desired depth of field, mind ND filter if too bright for
  a slow shutter at wide aperture
- ISO: Auto within a capped range to avoid visible noise in shadows
- AF: AF-C, Face/Eye tracking for talking-head or subject-following shots
- Other: SteadyShot "Active" if lens has OSS + IBIS combo; watch for
  focus breathing on the specific lens in use

## Street / walk-around

- Mode: A or P
- Aperture: f/4–f/5.6 for reasonable depth of field and forgiving focus
- Shutter: Auto-min shutter speed set to avoid motion blur on people
- ISO: Auto, cap ~6400
- AF: AF-C, Wide or Zone; Eye AF on for candid portraits
- Drive: Single, occasionally Lo continuous for decisive-moment sequences
